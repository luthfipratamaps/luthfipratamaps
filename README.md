## 👨‍💻 About Me

I build **end-to-end embedded and industrial IoT systems**, taking projects from an initial real-world problem through engineering design, implementation, validation, and deployment.

My work spans the **full engineering lifecycle — turning real-world problems into validated, deployable systems**:

```text
Problem Analysis
      ↓
Solution Architecture
      ↓
Component & Datasheet Research
      ↓
Schematic & PCB Design
      ↓
Embedded Firmware
      ↓
Calibration & Accuracy Validation
      ↓
System Testing & Documentation
      ↓
Connectivity & Telemetry
      ↓
Backend / Cloud Integration
      ↓
Deployment & Field Validation
```

I enjoy working at the boundaries between **electronics, firmware, communication systems, measurement, and software**, where every layer has to work reliably as part of one complete system.
